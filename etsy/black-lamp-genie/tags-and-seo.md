| Slot | What to put | Examples |

## Building a 13-tag set

| Count | Role | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 3–4 | Niche | funny food shirt, food label tee, nutrition facts shirt |
| 3–4 | Product / intent | funny graphic tee, unisex funny shirt, gag gift tee |
| 3–4 | Gift / audience | foodie gift, birthday gift men, kitchen humor tee |
| 1–2 | Design-specific | hot sauce shirt, expired but fine |
| 1 | Brand | black lamp genie |

**Rules**

- Exactly 13 tags on Etsy.  
- Prefer multi-word phrases shoppers type.  
- No competitor brand names. No trademarked food brands.  
- Don’t repeat the exact same phrase as the whole title; overlap words is fine.

## Description SEO tips

1. First two lines = searchable hook (design name + “funny food label shirt”).  
2. Repeat the core phrase once more in THE DESIGN — not five times.  
3. Spell out gift occasions; Etsy indexes description text.  
4. Close with brand line: *Black Lamp Genie — wishes granted in soft cotton.*  
5. Keep claims honest (fit, materials, processing time).

---

## Quick title checker

Ask before publish:

1. Would a stranger search this phrase on Etsy?  
2. Is the joke clear without a photo?  
3. Is **Black Lamp Genie** at the end?  
4. Under 140 characters?  
5. Free of real brand names?
